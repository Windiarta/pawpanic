📖 HOW TO PLAY PAW PANIC!

🔑

1. Grab Key & Unlock Exit Door

Cooperate to grab the Golden Key and unlock the door. Both cats must enter to clear the level!

🐱

2. Stack & Jump on Friend's Head

Jump on top of your partner's head to reach higher ledges neither can reach alone.

📦

3. Heavy Push Blocks [2]

Blocks labeled [2] require both players pushing together in the same direction!

🔘

4. Switches & Sync Buttons

Red buttons must be held down, green buttons toggle permanently, and blue buttons must be pressed simultaneously.

🪢

5. Elastic Tether Rope (Tier 3)

Both cats are tied together! Don't wander too far apart or you will pull each other into pitfalls.
